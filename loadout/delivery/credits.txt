so anyone can check where each file came from. Downloaded 2026-11-11 from
the makers' own sites only (for Quaternius, the download links on the
maker's own pack pages).

Maker:    Quaternius (quaternius.com)
Pack:     Ultimate Modular Men (February 2022), "Ultimate Modular Males by
          @Quaternius" in the pack's own License.txt
Page:     https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularcharacters.html
Download: by the owner, 2026-11-11, through the download link on that page
          (the maker's Google Drive folder)
License:  "License:
          CC0 1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0)
          Public Domain Dedication
          https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/"
          (License.txt in the pack)
Files:    models/people/crew-a.glb, models/people/crew-b.glb (the two
          delivery people), made from the pack's glTF characters
          Individual Characters/glTF/Casual_2.gltf (body, legs and feet,
          used for both), Suit.gltf (the head of the first person) and
          Adventurer.gltf (the head of the second person)
Changes:  repacked as binary glTF by a small script. Unused animations were
          removed: of the 24 clips only Walk and Idle_Neutral are kept, and
          only in crew-a.glb; crew-b.glb has the same skeleton and is
          animated with those clips by bone name. Unused parts were removed:
          the other heads, outfits, the backpack and the weapon props, the
          texture coordinates (no material in the pack uses a texture), and
          every accessor and byte range only those used. The skeleton, rest
          pose, skin weights and the kept clips are byte for byte the
          pack's own. In the scene the shirt, jeans, shoes, hair and one
          skin tone are recolored in code (charcoal polo shirts and blue
          jeans); a collar, a button placket and a small blue chest mark
          are added as plain shapes; arms, legs and the nod are posed by
          code on top of the clips.
